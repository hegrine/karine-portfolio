About the artisan

Patience, precision and a love of detail

Karine designs and hand-crafts beadwork and ceramics, from Fabergé-style eggs and jewellery to New Year ornaments and flowers made of paper and beads.

For eight years she led children's workshops, building step-by-step lessons that grow fine motor skills and confidence. An earlier career in clinical laboratories and food quality control brings a safety-first mindset and exacting attention to detail to every piece.
